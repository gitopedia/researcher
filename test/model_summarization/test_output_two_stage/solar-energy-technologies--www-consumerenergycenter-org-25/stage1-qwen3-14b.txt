### **3. Types of Solar Panels and Technologies**  
- **Monocrystalline vs. Polycrystalline Panels**:  
  - **Monocrystalline**: Higher efficiency but more expensive.  
  - **Polycrystalline**: Lower cost but slightly less efficient.  
- **Energy Storage Solutions**:  
  - **Battery Systems** (e.g., lithium-ion): Enhance energy independence and optimize utility bill savings.  
  - **Grid-Tied Systems**: Allow surplus energy to be sold back to the grid (net metering).

### **4. Factors to Consider When Choosing Solar Energy**  
- **Cost and Efficiency**:  
  - Initial investment in panels and storage systems affects return on investment.  
  - Efficiency varies based on panel type and technology.  
- **Location and Climate**:  
  - **Sunlight Exposure**: Regions with long, sunny days yield higher energy output.  
  - **Temperature**: Cooler climates improve panel performance; excessive heat reduces efficiency.  
  - **Geographical Features**: Urban areas may face shading challenges, while rural areas benefit from unobstructed sunlight.  
- **Incentives and Rebates**: Local programs can reduce upfront costs, making solar a smart investment.
