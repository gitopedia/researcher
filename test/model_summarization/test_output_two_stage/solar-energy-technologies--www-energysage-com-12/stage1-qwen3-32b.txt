# History and Development of Solar Energy

## Timeline of Major Events in Solar Energy History

### 1954: Silicon Photovoltaic Cell Invention
- **Bell Labs** (Daryl Chapin, Calvin Fuller, Gerald Pearson) developed the first practical silicon photovoltaic (PV) cell with 4% efficiency.
- Marked the beginning of modern solar technology, enabling solar cells to power electronic devices.

### 1958: Solar Power in Space
- **Vanguard I satellite** became the first spacecraft to use solar panels (1 watt) to power its radios.
- Subsequent satellites (Vanguard II, Explorer III, Sputnik-3) incorporated PV technology.

### 1964: First Solar-Powered Satellite
- **NASA's Nimbus spacecraft** operated entirely on a 470-watt solar array.

### 1966: Orbiting Astronomical Observatory
- **NASA's first Orbiting Astronomical Observatory** used a 1-kilowatt solar array.

### 1973: First Solar Residence
- **University of Delaware** constructed "Solar One," the first solar-powered building using hybrid solar thermal and PV systems.
- Featured **building-integrated photovoltaics (BIPV)**, embedding solar technology into the roof.

### 1981: Solar-Powered Aircraft
- **Paul MacCready** built the **Solar Challenger**, the first solar-powered aircraft, which flew across the English Channel.

### 1985: Efficiency Milestone
- **University of New South Wales** achieved 20% efficiency for silicon solar cells.

### 1998: High-Altitude Solar Flight
- NASA's **Pathfinder** solar aircraft reached 80,000 feet, setting an altitude record.

### 2001: Record-Breaking Altitude
- NASA's non-rocket aircraft reached 96,000 feet, surpassing the Pathfinder record.

### 2016: Global Solar Flight
- **Solar Impulse 2**, piloted by **Bertrand Piccard**, completed the first zero-emissions circumnavigation of the globe.

### 2016: Efficiency Breakthrough
- **University of New South Wales** achieved 34.5% efficiency for silicon solar cells.

## Political and Cultural Milestones

### 1979–1981: White House Solar Panels
- **President Jimmy Carter** installed solar panels on the White House.
- **President Ronald Reagan** removed them in 1981.

### 2010: White House Solar Revival
- **President Barack Obama** had solar panels and a solar water heater reinstalled at the White House.

## Solar Technology Advancements

### Early Discoveries
- **1839**: French physicist **Edmond Becquerel** discovered the photovoltaic effect.
- **1873**: **Willoughby Smith** identified selenium's photoconductive properties.
- **1876**: **William Grylls Adams** and **Richard Evans Day** demonstrated selenium's ability to generate electricity from sunlight.
- **1883**: **Charles Fritts** created the first selenium-based solar cells.

### Efficiency Improvements
- **1957–1960**: Hoffman Electronics increased PV efficiency from 8% to 14%.
- **1999**: **NREL and SpectroLab Inc.** developed a 33.3% efficient solar cell.

## Cost Reduction Over Time

- **1956**: Solar panels cost **$300 per watt**.
- **1975**: Prices dropped to **$100 per watt**.
- **Present**: Solar panels cost as low as **$0.50 per watt**.
- **Annual Decline**: Solar prices have decreased by **10% per year since 1980**.
- **Impact**: Driven demand, leading to over **1 million U.S. installations by 2016**.

## Solar Applications Beyond Electricity

### Solar-Powered Transportation
- **Solar Impulse 2**: First solar-powered aircraft to circumnavigate the globe (2016).
- **Solar Challenger**: First solar aircraft to cross the English Channel (1981).

### Space Exploration
- Solar arrays power satellites and spacecraft, enabling long-duration missions.
