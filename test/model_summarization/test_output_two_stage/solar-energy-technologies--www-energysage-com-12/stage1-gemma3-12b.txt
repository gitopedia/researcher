**II. History of Solar Energy**

*   **Ancient Uses (Pre-Photovoltaics):**
    *   **7th Century B.C.:** Humans used sunlight to light fires with magnifying glasses.
    *   **3rd Century B.C.:** Greeks and Romans used mirrors to light torches for religious ceremonies ("burning mirrors").
    *   **20 A.D.:** Chinese civilization also used mirrors for the same purpose.
    *   **1700s-1800s:** Sunlight used to power ovens for voyages and solar-powered steamboats.
    *   **Sunrooms:** Ancient Romans utilized sunrooms (massive windows) to direct sunlight into buildings.
    *   **Anasazi (Pueblo Native Americans):** Built homes on cliffs facing south to capture sunlight for warmth.
*   **Early Scientific Discoveries:**
    *   **Edmond Becquerel (1839):** Discovered the photovoltaic effect – light increases electricity generation in a conducting solution.
    *   **William Grylls Adams & Richard Evans Day (1876):** Demonstrated electricity generation from selenium exposed to sunlight.
    *   **Charles Fritts (1883):** Created the first selenium solar cell, considered the first photovoltaic device.
*   **Modern Developments:**
    *   **1956:** Hoffman Electronics improved photovoltaic efficiency from 8% to 14%.
    *   **1973:** Construction of the first solar building, "Solar One," incorporating both solar thermal and photovoltaic power.
    *   **1985:** University of New South Wales achieved 20% efficiency for silicon cells.
    *   **1999:** National Renewable Energy Laboratory achieved 33.3% efficiency.
    *   **2016:** University of New South Wales broke the efficiency record with 34.5%.
*   **Notable Projects:**
    *   **1981:** Solar Challenger aircraft flew across the English Channel powered by solar energy.
    *   **1998:** Pathfinder aircraft reached 80,000 feet.
    *   **2001:** NASA aircraft reached 96,000 feet.
    *   **2016:** Solar Impulse 2 completed the first zero-emissions flight worldwide.
*   **Government Involvement:**
    *   **1979:** President Jimmy Carter installed solar panels on the White House.
    *   **2010:** President Barack Obama requested the reinstallation of solar panels on the White House.
*   **Cost Trends:** Solar panel costs have decreased significantly over time, dropping from roughly $300 per watt in 1956 to under $0.50 per watt today.
