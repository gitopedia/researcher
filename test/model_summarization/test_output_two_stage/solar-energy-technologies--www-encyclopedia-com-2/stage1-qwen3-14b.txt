### **3. Solar Heating and Cooling Systems**
- **Passive Systems**:
  - **Direct Gain**: South-facing glass allows sunlight to heat thermal mass (floors, walls).
  - **Thermal Storage Wall**: Absorbs heat during the day and releases it at night.
  - **Solar Greenhouse**: Combines direct gain and thermal storage for heating.
  - **Roof Pond**: Uses water containers for thermal storage with movable insulation.
  - **Convective Loop**: Uses natural convection to circulate heated air (e.g., thermosiphons).
- **Active Systems**:
  - **Flat-Plate Collectors**: Absorb solar radiation to heat water or air.
  - **Evacuated Tube Collectors**: Achieve higher temperatures (up to 177°C) via vacuum insulation.
  - **Parabolic-Trough Collectors**: Focus sunlight to reach temperatures up to 400°C (requires sun tracking).
  - **Hybrid Systems**: Combine passive and active features for year-round efficiency.
