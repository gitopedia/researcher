# Solar Energy: History, Technology, and Applications

## Historical Background

- Solar energy has been utilized by ancient civilizations for centuries, used for heating, cooking, and lighting.
- Early solar technologies focused on harnessing solar energy for heating water and buildings.
- Solar-powered steam engines and solar distillation techniques were also developed historically.
- The discovery of the photovoltaic effect by Edmond Becquerel marked a significant milestone, leading to the development of the first practical solar cells.
- Solar cells initially found use in satellites and space exploration.

## Key Concepts and Definitions

- **Photovoltaic (PV) technology:** A method of generating electricity directly from sunlight using semiconductors.
- **Solar thermal technology:**  A technology that uses solar energy to heat water or air.
- **Net metering:** A billing mechanism that credits solar energy system owners for the electricity they add to the grid.
- **Solar efficiency:**  A measure of how effectively a solar panel converts sunlight into electricity.
- **Solar photovoltaic cells:** Semiconductor devices that convert sunlight into electricity.
