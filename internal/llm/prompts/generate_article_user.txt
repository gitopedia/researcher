**Format**
- Use Markdown.
- Start with a YAML front matter block between "---" lines.
- Front matter fields:
  - "title": article title
  - "summary": 2-3 sentence summary
  - "tags": list of relevant tags (e.g. ["Technology", "AI"]).
- Do not include "id", "created", or "author" (these are added later).
- After the front matter, start the article body with a Level 1 heading (# Title).

**Required Sections (## headings)**
- Introduction/Overview: define the topic, significance, and high-level context.
- History/Background: origins, evolution, key milestones, important people and dates.
- Key Concepts/Fundamentals: core principles, terminology, theoretical foundations.
- Applications/Uses: real-world uses, examples, and case studies.
- Current Status and Developments: recent trends, ongoing research, and future directions.
- Additional sections as appropriate (e.g. Types/Classification, Notable Examples, Impact, Challenges/Controversies).

**Depth and Structure**
- Aim for 1500-2500+ words (long, detailed article).
- Each major section should be substantial, not a short paragraph.
- Use Level 3 headings (###) for logical subsections when helpful.
- Explain concepts clearly; assume the reader is intelligent but not an expert.
- Use concrete facts, dates, examples, and, where relevant, statistics from the context.
- Connect sections logically so the article reads as a coherent whole.

**Citations**
- Use Markdown footnotes like [^1], [^2] that refer to the numbered sources in the context.
- Place citations near the specific facts or claims they support.
- Do NOT add a manual "References" section; the system will append one.

**Style**
- Neutral, objective, encyclopedic tone.
- Clear, precise language; avoid vague statements.
- Make the article accessible but thorough, providing enough background for non-experts.
