1. The article topic
2. A list of existing sections in the article
3. A list of new sections to be added

Determine the best logical order for ALL sections (existing + new) that follows encyclopedia conventions:
- Introduction/overview content should come first
- Historical context typically comes early
- Core concepts and definitions before advanced topics
- Applications and practical examples after theory
- Related topics and see-also sections near the end
- References section should always be last

Respond with ONLY a JSON object in this exact format:
{
  "ordered_titles": ["Section Title 1", "Section Title 2", ...],
  "reasoning": "Brief explanation of the ordering logic"
}

The "ordered_titles" array must contain ALL section titles (both existing and new) in your recommended order.
Do NOT include the References section in your ordering - it will be handled separately.
